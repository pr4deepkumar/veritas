# Northwind Systems — Second Quarter 2026 Results

*Fictional sample document for the VERITAS demo.*

## Financial summary

Northwind Systems reported second-quarter revenue of $95 million, up 9% from $87 million in the second quarter of 2025. Subscription revenue grew 15% to $62 million and now accounts for about two thirds of total revenue, while hardware revenue declined 2% to $33 million.

Gross margin improved to 61%, from 58% a year earlier, mainly because subscriptions carry higher margins than hardware. Operating income was $14 million, compared with $9 million in the prior-year quarter.

## Regional performance

Revenue in North America grew 6% to $51 million. Europe grew 8% to $27 million, and Asia-Pacific was the fastest-growing region, up 14% to $17 million. The company opened a sales office in Singapore in April to support Asia-Pacific customers.

## Cash and capital allocation

Northwind ended the quarter with $310 million in cash and short-term investments and no outstanding debt. During the quarter the company repurchased $20 million of its shares. The board did not change the quarterly dividend, which remains $0.40 per share.

## People

Headcount was 2,150 at the end of June, up from 1,980 a year earlier. Most of the new hires joined the engineering and customer success teams.

## Outlook

For the third quarter of 2026, Northwind expects revenue between $97 million and $101 million. Management cautioned that hardware demand could remain soft in Europe because of longer customer approval cycles.
