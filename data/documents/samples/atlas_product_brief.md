# Atlas Analytics Suite — Product Brief

*Fictional sample document for the VERITAS demo.*

## Overview

Atlas is Northwind's analytics suite for operations teams. It combines dashboards, scheduled reports and anomaly alerts on top of a customer's existing data warehouse, without copying the data into a separate system.

## Availability

A public beta opened in May 2026 with 140 participating customers. General availability is planned for the third quarter of 2026. Atlas will be offered in two tiers: Team, at $49 per user per month, and Enterprise, priced by contract.

## Features

- Dashboards that refresh every five minutes, or in real time on the Enterprise tier.
- Anomaly alerts that compare each metric with its typical weekly pattern and notify users by email or chat.
- Scheduled PDF reports delivered daily, weekly or monthly.
- Role-based access control and single sign-on on both tiers; audit logs on the Enterprise tier only.

## Security and compliance

Atlas runs in Northwind's existing data centers in Virginia and Oregon. A third data center, in Dublin, Ireland, is scheduled to go live in 2027 for customers who need their data stored in the European Union. Northwind's platform is certified to ISO 27001 and completed its first SOC 2 Type II audit in 2025.

## Beta results

Beta customers reported that Atlas cut the time spent preparing weekly operations reports from about six hours to under one hour. The beta did not measure the effect on decision quality.
