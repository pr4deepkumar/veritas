# Northwind Systems — Employee Benefits Policy (2026)

*Fictional sample document for the VERITAS demo.*

## Parental leave

Employees receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child. Leave can be taken in one block or in up to three separate periods, as long as all of it is used within the first year. Employees on parental leave keep their full health coverage.

## Learning stipend

Full-time employees are eligible for an annual learning stipend of $1,000 for courses, certifications, books and conference tickets. Part-time employees receive a prorated stipend based on their contracted hours. Unused stipend does not carry over to the next calendar year.

## Hybrid work

Most roles follow a hybrid schedule with two or three office days per week, agreed with the employee's manager. Fully remote arrangements require approval from a department head and are reviewed every twelve months.

## Retirement plan

The company matches employee retirement contributions dollar for dollar up to 4% of base salary. Matching contributions vest immediately.

## Travel

All international business travel requires pre-approval from the employee's manager and the finance team at least two weeks before departure. Domestic trips under $1,500 only need manager approval.
